# Changelog

## 1.1.4

- Keep automatic injury substitutions within the four-foreigner limit, avoiding
  an illegal lineup that could block the second half.
- Wait for the saved sponsorship notice before testing a browser reload.

## 1.1.3

- Use a real screenshot of matches in progress for home-page and invitation
  sharing previews, with descriptive image text and a fresh image URL.

## 1.1.2

- Separate attacking and defensive quality while retaining every position's
  contribution to both. Use relative strength and balanced formation tempo so
  strong squads have a sustained advantage without removing match upsets.
- Choose scorers by position, strength, fitness and morale; exclude goalkeepers
  and require exactly one goalkeeper in the starting eleven.
- Add mathematical properties and repeated-season simulations for match balance,
  exceptional forwards, formation tradeoffs, scoring variance and title odds.

## 1.1.1

- Link to the source repository from the home-screen footer with a pixel-style
  GitHub icon, DOS border and keyboard/touch support.

## 1.1.0

- Give players permanent hidden talent, smooth youth development and accelerating
  decline after 33. Teammate influence changes current ability without erasing
  individual potential, with a one-time migration for existing careers.
- Sustain division strength levels and strong auction supply through balanced
  academy cohorts, useful AI recruitment and real surplus-player transfers.
- Give AI clubs operating budgets, sustainable ticket prices and stadium upkeep;
  reserve season funding before transfers or construction. Extend 50-season
  checks to talent, division strength, market liquidity and exact accounting.
- Match employment offers to the manager's recent performance and division.

- Add rare, age-appropriate real players arriving from abroad after regular
  auctions, usually returning Brazilians, with saved draws and normal bidding.
  Account for fees paid abroad and balance academy intake against imported players.
- Let managers choose their academy graduates before each new season, with fixed
  positional quotas, saved candidate pools and automatic multiplayer choices
  when the decision timer expires.
- Retire older players at season renewal with an exponentially increasing
  chance after 33, show affected squads a retirement notice, and replenish
  vacancies from academies without shrinking or inflating the player population.
- Show only formations supported by the available squad, and apply the same
  restrictions to keyboard shortcuts and multiplayer submissions.
- Show the managed club's division and the upcoming opponent's own position and
  points on the club screen.
- Add three annual sponsorship offers after the next-season club decision,
  priced by division, sócios, league results and awards, with 30 fictional brands.
- Keep the offer screen focused on the brands and payment terms.
- Offer three non-betting brands while bets are banned, including pending offers
  resumed from older saves.
- Offer fixed income and win/draw bonuses across 14 league rounds, plus richer
  betting deals whose government-ban risk reduces expected returns by about 10%.
- Show suspension/reinstatement on a separate screen after standings, only to
  affected managers; preserve contracts and government decisions in saves and
  support multiplayer choices.
- Extend long-term economy checks with sponsorship accounting and 50,000
  financial-season trials; verify the offer and finance screens on desktop/phone.

## 1.0.1

- Rebalance transfer fees so strong players are attainable on lower-division budgets.
- Increase league and Copa do Brasil prizes, add league runner-up awards, and pay
  eliminated cup teams half the advancing team's round prize.
- Show recorded prize payments after cup rounds and on the season-end screen,
  including all league runners-up and the three Cr$ 200,000 individual awards.
- Add four 50-season economy simulations covering price inflation, wages, buyer
  availability, cash accounting, and repeated transfers.
- Keep long careers loadable when players age past 70 or injuries leave an AI
  selection with fewer than eleven available starters.

## 1.0.0

- Portuguese DOS-style football management with four divisions, Copa do Brasil,
  Libertadores, transfers, contracts, stadium finances, and multi-season careers.
- Solo browser saves and authoritative casual multiplayer for up to eight managers.
- Keyboard and touch controls, portrait layouts, invitations, chat, and reconnects.
- Configurable hosting paths, versioned release archives, checksum verification,
  staged installation, upgrades, and rollback.
- Strict TypeScript, unused-code checks, deterministic simulations, transport
  tests, and desktop/mobile browser verification.

This is an independent remake in tribute to the original Elifoot II by André
Elias, with no affiliation or endorsement. Original application code is
available under MIT.
